Dear Sir Salman,

I don't really know how to put everything I feel into words, but I wanted to create something that could hold a little piece of my gratitude.

You were not just someone who taught in a classroom. You were someone whose words, patience and way of teaching made a difference.

Maybe this website is small, but the appreciation behind it is not.

“The best teachers leave footprints in your heart long after the classroom is gone.”

Things I’ll Remember

The way you taught

You made lessons feel like more than just lessons. The things we learn from a good teacher stay with us.

The little moments

Sometimes it is not the big moments we remember. It is the small conversations, smiles and ordinary days.

The impact

Years from now, I may forget some chapters and formulas, but I don't think I will forget the teacher who made them meaningful.

♡

Thank You, Sir.

For every lesson.
For every bit of patience.
For every moment that made a difference.

Wherever life takes us, I hope you know that you were appreciated, remembered, and genuinely valued.


— From a student who will always be grateful
